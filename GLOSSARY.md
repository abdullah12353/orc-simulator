# ORC NONSENSE

A private, persistent world of spectacularly stupid masked orc captains. Friends meddle in it from the outside and laugh at what it does.

## People and orcs

**Player**:
A friend who meddles in the world from outside. A Player never controls an orc directly.
_Avoid_: User, gamer, commander

**Captain**:
A named, persistent orc with a portrait, voice, personality and history. The cast of the world. All Captains are male.
_Avoid_: Orc (when you mean a specific named one), NPC, unit, character

**Krúg**:
The founding Captain. Spelled with an accent on the u and pronounced "KROOG" (long u), never "Groog".

**Trait**:
A personality quirk from a pool shared by every Captain, such as Glutton or Mummy's Boy. Each Captain has exactly two. Captains get their jokes from Traits, never from their accent.
_Avoid_: Perk, type, personality (as the term)

**Fear**:
Something a Captain panics at or can be made to give in to, such as water or his mum. Each Captain starts with one, and Humiliations can add more.
_Avoid_: Weakness, phobia (as the term)

**Epithet**:
The title tacked onto a Captain's name, such as "the Damp". A Captain has at most one at a time, and it is usually earned through disaster.
_Avoid_: Nickname, title (when you mean the epithet)

**Accent archetype**:
The regional or class voice a Captain speaks in, such as cockney or posh RP. It is his voice, never his joke.
_Avoid_: Race, nationality, accent type

**Warlord**:
The Captain currently ruling the fortress.
_Avoid_: Ruler, overlord, boss

## Influence

**Meddle**:
A single outside intervention a Player makes in the world, such as planting a Rumour.
_Avoid_: Action, move, command

**Rumour**:
A Meddle that plants a claim about one Captain, supposedly said by another. It may work, reach the wrong orc, or get Garbled.
_Avoid_: Gossip, lie

**Claim**:
The kind of thing a Rumour asserts: an insult, a Fear exposed, a theft, a mum claim or a coup plot.
_Avoid_: Topic, content

**Garble**:
A Rumour mutating on its way to the target, always escalating into something worse.
_Avoid_: Misunderstanding, distortion

**Dare**:
A Meddle that pushes a Captain towards a stunt (a Stunt dare) or a prank on another Captain (a Prank dare). He botches it, pulls it off, or refuses.
_Avoid_: Challenge, quest, order

**Backing**:
A Player putting gold behind a Captain and leaving it there. It slightly nudges that Captain's chances in a Clash and pays out on both wins and Infamy.
_Avoid_: Sponsorship, bet, funding

**Stake**:
The gold a Player has put behind one Captain. It stays put, and survives the Captain's death, until the Player withdraws it.
_Avoid_: Bet, wager, investment

**Stipend**:
The small, equal daily allowance of gold every Player receives.
_Avoid_: Income, salary, daily reward

**Stable**:
The set of Captains a Player currently backs. Stables may overlap.
_Avoid_: Team, roster

**Blame**:
The lasting record linking a Player to the consequences of their Meddles. It is announced, never ranked.
_Avoid_: Score, points, credit

**Accusation**:
A Captain naming a Player as the cause of their misfortune. Often wrong, and separate from Blame.
_Avoid_: Blame (when the orc is speaking), grudge (against a Player)

**Rap sheet**:
A Player's unranked list of their most infamous Blame.
_Avoid_: Profile, stats, leaderboard

## Voice

**The Gutter Herald**:
The world's deadpan tabloid. It reports what happened while you were away, treating orc idiocy with the gravity of history. Called "the Herald" for short.
_Avoid_: Mordor Morning Herald, daily report, newsfeed

**Day zero**:
The simulated day the sim pre-rolls when a world is created, so the first edition has stories in it.
_Avoid_: Tutorial, warm-up

**Edition**:
One Player's "Since You Left" issue of the Herald, covering the time since their last visit.
_Avoid_: Issue, report, digest

**Stop press**:
The strip of new template lines shown above the last edition when a new one isn't due yet.
_Avoid_: Update, refresh

**Clipping**:
The shareable card cut from the Herald after a Clash: headline, portraits, the loser's last line as a pull quote, and the Blame line.
_Avoid_: Share card, poster, screenshot

**Swap test**:
The content rule for accents. A Captain's accent is their voice, never their punchline: if swapping the accent kills the joke, the joke is cut.
_Avoid_: Accent humour

## Consequences

**Clash**:
A short, theatrical two-portrait encounter between Captains. The rules decide who wins.
_Avoid_: Fight, battle, duel, Idiot Arena (as a term for one encounter)

**Template**:
One event as data: its trigger, cast, outcome odds, effects, line variants and base comedy weight. Clashes and Dares are templates too.
_Avoid_: Script, scenario, event type

**Comedy weight**:
A deterministic score for how funny an event is, computed by the sim from its Facts. It ranks the Herald and feeds the Hall of Shame.
_Avoid_: Score, rating, priority

**Callback**:
An event that reuses something the world already remembers: an existing Grudge tag, a Trophy changing hands again, a Fear already shown. Callbacks score extra comedy weight.
_Avoid_: Reference, repeat

**Grudge**:
A Captain's lasting memory of something another Captain did to them. It shapes their future encounters.
_Avoid_: Feud, beef (as terms)

**Trophy**:
An item a Captain takes from a defeated Captain and visibly keeps.
_Avoid_: Loot, drop

**Return**:
A dead Captain coming back, changed by a Humiliation.
_Avoid_: Respawn, resurrection

**Benched**:
Moved from the active 12 to the reserve to make room for a Returning Captain. It happens to the active Captain with the least recent Infamy.
_Avoid_: Retired, dropped, sacked (as the term)

**Humiliation**:
A lasting, visible mark of shame a Captain gains on Return or through disaster, such as bandages, a missing limb, a disguise or an epithet.
_Avoid_: Debuff, injury (as the general term)

**Infamy**:
How renowned a Captain is for stupidity. It is separate from Power and decides succession to Warlord.
_Avoid_: Fame, reputation, notoriety

**Unmasked**:
A disguised Captain being exposed, which ends his disguise. It is its own notable event.
_Avoid_: Revealed, caught

**Legendary**:
The permanent status of a Captain whose lifetime Infamy has passed the Legendary threshold. He keeps playing, with a stamp on his portrait and Introductions.
_Avoid_: Hero, legend (lowercase, as the status), retired

**Legendary deed**:
The event that takes a Captain over the Legendary threshold. It makes the Herald's front page and the Hall of Shame.
_Avoid_: Achievement, feat

**Power**:
How dangerous a Captain actually is in a Clash.
_Avoid_: Level, strength, rank
