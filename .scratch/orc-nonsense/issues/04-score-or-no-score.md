# Score or no score

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Mohammed
Blocked by: (none)

## Question

Should Players have any score, standings or win state, or is ORC NONSENSE pure stirring with no score? If there's something in between (e.g. a Hall of Shame attributing disasters to the Player whose Rumour caused them, but no ranking), what exactly is it?

This decides what Backing pays out *into*, and whether Players have any reason to work against each other.

## Answer

Resolved with Mohammed on 2026-10-29 (three grilling rounds).

**No score, but loud Blame.**

- **No win state.** No seasons, standings or leaderboard. Seasons stay reserved for later. A win state invites optimising, and optimising kills the stupidity.
- **Blame** (new glossary term) replaces score: the lasting record of which Player's Meddles caused which consequences. Announced, never ranked.
  - It follows the **whole causal chain** (Rumour → Grudge → Clash → death → Return → coup). The Herald can trace it back ("It all began when Mohammed whispered about a bucket…").
  - It's **shared** between co-conspirators, including a Player who meant to help.
  - **Only Meddles produce Blame.** Backing links a Player to a Captain ("Haris's champion"), so narration can mention it, but a backed Captain's own stupidity is never the backer's Blame. Backers are rewarded through the Infamy payout instead.
- **Rap sheet** (new glossary term): each Player has an unranked list of their most infamous Blame. Nothing is compared side by side.
- **Gold is fuel, not score.** It's spending power that is never shown as wealth or compared between Players. Sources, caps and resets → [Backing economy](08-backing-economy.md).
- **Open Stables.** Stables can overlap and any Captain can be targeted. Rivalry between friends comes through the orcs and through Blame.
- **Accusation** (new glossary term): rarely, a Captain names a Player as the cause of their misfortune. This deliberately punches a small hole in the fourth wall.
  - **Often wrong** (about half the time), sometimes naming a Player with no connection at all. The true Blame can be shown alongside it.
  - **Mangled names**: each Player has a small fixed set of orc pronunciations ("MO-HAM-UD THE WHISPERER", "'Arris"), pre-rendered once as audio.
  - **Flavour only**: the sim picks the moment and the name as a Fact. No Player-directed Grudges, penalties or other state changes. "Outside interference only" still holds, because Players still can't act inside the world.
  - **Rare**: only at high-Infamy beats (a Return Introduction, a death line, a new Warlord's first words), and at most about one per Herald.

**Reserved for later iterations:** Blame that starts anonymous and is revealed later · recurring Herald awards ("Meddler of the Week") · Accusations with mechanical effects (e.g. the accuser doing worse when backed by the accused).

**Effects on the map:** [Backing economy](08-backing-economy.md) is unblocked. The Herald fog gains Blame tracing and Accusation slots, the Hall of Shame fog gains the rap sheet, and the data model fog must record Meddle → consequence causal links.
