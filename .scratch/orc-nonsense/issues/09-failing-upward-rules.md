# Failing Upward rules

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Assignee: Mohammed
Blocked by: 06

## Question

What are the rules for death, Return, Humiliation, Infamy, rank and becoming Legendary?

- How does a Captain die (Clash only, or accidents too)? How long until they Return? Does every Captain Return?
- The Humiliation table: categories (bandage, limb, disguise, epithet, new fear), how they stack, and how they show on the portrait and in the voice (e.g. muffled barks).
- How Infamy is earned and spent. Rank tiers below Warlord, if any. The succession trigger.
- The Legendary threshold and what a Legendary deed is. Does Legendary retire a Captain into the Hall of Shame?

## Comments

- From [Backing economy](08-backing-economy.md): Infamy gains directly set Backing payouts (1% of the Stake per point). Aim for roughly 5–30 Infamy per notable disaster.
