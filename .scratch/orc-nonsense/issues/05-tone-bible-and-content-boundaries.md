# Tone bible and content boundaries

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: Mohammed
Blocked by: 03

## Question

What is the house voice of ORC NONSENSE, and where (if anywhere) are its limits?

- Mohammed wants "all out, no censorship", in the register of "a day in the life of a Brexit geezer". The supplementary note says "accents should add character without relying on ethnic caricatures". Which wins, and what does that mean concretely for the Indian-accented Captain, among others?
- Given what [AI and voice costs](03-ai-and-voice-costs.md) finds about model and TTS content policies, which content should be **hand-written** (barks, catchphrases, Introductions) and which can be **generated**?
- Swearing level, crude and sexual humour, real people and politics. Is Brexit a *register* or a literal topic?
- An original name for the daily report ("Mordor" is Tolkien's).
- 3–5 sample lines per accent archetype, to calibrate.

## Answer

Resolved with Mohammed on 2026-10-29 (two grilling rounds plus a calibration round). Full rules and approved samples: [tone-bible.md](../tone-bible.md).

- **Which wins:** everything goes except punching at identity. Swearing, crudeness, cruelty between orcs and class mockery are all fine. Race, religion and nationality are never the joke. The same rule covers hand-written and generated lines.
- **Accents:** the **swap test**. An accent is a voice, never a punchline. The Indian-English Captain keeps the accent and gets a trait from the shared pool, the same as everyone else. Accents are written down through word choice and grammar only, with no phonetic respelling (muffled is the exception).
- **Heat:** full British profanity. The c-word is allowed in banter but rare (about one per Clash at most). No slurs, including stand-in "orc slurs". Sexual humour stops at *Carry On* innuendo and "your mum" jokes.
- **Brexit** is a register, not a topic. No real people, politicians, parties or news.
- **Cast:** all Captains are male. Women exist only as off-screen running gags, never shown or voiced: Krúg's mum, and Susan (the Brexit-geezer meme's wife). "Once you go black" is re-grounded onto black pudding, tar or soot.
- **Who writes what:** hand-written for everything prerecorded (Introductions, catchphrases, barks, stingers, Accusations) and for the edgiest material. Generated for Clash dialogue and the Herald, with the tone bible as the system prompt and its samples as few-shot examples. Templated fallbacks sit behind everything generated.
- **Voices:** the orcs talk in sweary pub banter. The Herald is a clean, deadpan red-top. It may mock Players' Meddles, but never the Players themselves.
- **Name:** the daily report is **The Gutter Herald** ("the Herald").
- **Samples:** 3–4 approved lines each for cockney, RP, West Country, Scouse, Glaswegian, Indian English and muffled, plus Herald headlines.

**Effects on the map:**

- [Captain card and name generator](06-captain-card-and-name-generator.md) inherits the calibration roster, the all-male cast and the swap test for traits.
- The worked example's Lady Snarlbottom is now **Lord Snarlbottom**.
- Voice casting is added to the fog.
