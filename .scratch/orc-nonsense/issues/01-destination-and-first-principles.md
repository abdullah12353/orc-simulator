# Destination and first principles

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: (none)

## Question

What is this wayfinder effort finding its way to? And how do we settle the first-order tensions in the brainstorm: live vs away, money vs stupidity, content, Krúg, and who decides?

## Answer

Resolved with Mohammed on 2026-10-29 (first grilling round).

- **Destination**: a locked MVP spec, meaning the GDD plus decision log with every open question resolved. Building is a later, separate effort.
- **Cadence**: async-first. The world ticks slowly when nobody is on and speeds up while someone is watching. Time away is compressed into the Herald.
- **Competition**: *not settled*. Explore both a light scoreboard and pure no-score stirring. "We're not trying to beat the game, it's just to get us laughing too much." → [Score or no score](04-score-or-no-score.md)
- **Backing**: small edge on odds, pays out on wins *and* Infamy.
- **Succession**: the Warlord is chosen by Infamy, with Power as the tie-break.
- **Content**: Mohammed wants "all out, no censorship", in the register of "a day in the life of a Brexit geezer" ("exit means exit", "once you go black, you never go back"). This conflicts with the supplementary note's "accents without ethnic caricature", and what the narration model will produce also matters. → [Tone bible and content boundaries](05-tone-bible-and-content-boundaries.md)
- **Founding Captain**: **Krúg**, with an accent on the u, pronounced "KROOG" (long u), never "Groog".
- **Decision-maker**: Mohammed plans alone. No sign-off from Haris needed.
